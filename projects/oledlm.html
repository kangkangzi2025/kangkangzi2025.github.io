OLED-LM: Property-Targeted OLED Molecular Design via Language Modeling and Reinforcement Learning

Fukang Wen · January 2026 · Tags: Molecular Generation, OLED, LLM, Reinforcement Learning, GRPO

Overview

OLED-LM is a pipeline for generating novel OLED (Organic Light-Emitting Diode) molecules with desired photophysical properties, specifically the first singlet excitation energy \(S_1\) (emission color) and oscillator strength \(f\) (brightness). Molecules are represented as SMILES strings and generated autoregressively by a language model trained through three stages: pre-training, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement learning (RL).

Pipeline

  • Pre-training: A LLaMA-style causal Transformer is trained via next-token prediction on 9 million unlabeled SMILES strings, learning the distribution of valid OLED molecular structures.
  • Property Predictors: A BERT model is pre-trained with masked language modeling on the same corpus, then fine-tuned on 10K DFT-labeled molecules for \(S_1\) and \(f\) regression. Ablation studies compare BERT, ChemBERTa, and MolFormer backbones.
  • Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): The LLaMA model is fine-tuned for property-conditioned generation, where target \((S_1, f)\) values are encoded as a token prefix before the SMILES sequence. Training mixes conditional and unconditional objectives with curriculum learning.
  • GRPO Reinforcement Learning: Group Relative Policy Optimization further aligns the generator with target properties. The BERT predictors serve as reward models, scoring groups of candidate molecules. KL regularization prevents drift from the SFT policy. Ablation studies vary the KL coefficient, group size, and validity reward.
  • Validation: Generated molecules are checked via RDKit for chemical validity and verified by DFT calculations for property accuracy.
